How this works

Windows tile automatically - the first one takes the master column on the left, the rest stack down the right. Nothing overlaps and nothing gets lost behind anything else. Four windows fit per workspace; opening a fifth closes whichever you touched least recently.

Switch to floating with F if you would rather drag windows around and resize them from the corner.

Terminal

The terminal is real enough to be worth opening: help lists commands, ↑/↓ walk history, and Tab completes.
